# Facing (Skincare Website)

Current skincare apps offer features such as daily check-ins, product tracking, calendars, and AI analysis. Apps such as Dermaday use photos and AI to analyse skin and provide recommendations for skin concerns. Rather than telling users what to do, our site, Facing, works as a personal skin diary.

This allows users to:

1) Track their skin daily through ratings, notes, and common skin concerns.
2) Build a personal history using a calendar to see how their skin changes over time.
3) Track their products and routine consistency to understand their own skincare habits.
4) Identify patterns for themselves and make informed decisions about their routine.

We don't promote brands or tell users what products to use. Instead, we help young people understand their own routines, notice patterns, and build more consistent skincare habits.
